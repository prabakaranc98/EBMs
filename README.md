# EBMs

## Objective

This repository is a personal space for **exploration, learning, experimentation, and building** with **Energy-Based Models (EBMs)** and **JEPA (Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture)**-based architectures.

The goal is to:
- **Explore** the theoretical foundations and intuitions behind EBMs and JEPA
- **Learn** by working through papers, implementations, and resources in this space
- **Experiment** with different formulations, training objectives, and architectural choices
- **Build** practical implementations and prototypes grounded in EBM and JEPA principles

## What are EBMs and JEPA?

**Energy-Based Models (EBMs)** are a class of generative/discriminative models that learn a scalar energy function over the input space, assigning low energy to observed/valid configurations and high energy to others. They offer a flexible framework for modeling complex distributions without requiring explicit normalization.

**JEPA (Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture)**, popularized by Yann LeCun's work on autonomous machine intelligence, is a self-supervised learning framework where a model learns to predict representations of the world in an abstract embedding space rather than predicting raw pixels. JEPA-style architectures are closely related to EBMs and are seen as a promising path toward more efficient and robust world models.
